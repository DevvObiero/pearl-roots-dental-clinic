Treatments we offer

Dental implants

A dental implant is a small post placed in the jawbone where a tooth used to be. Over time the bone heals around it, giving a firm base for the replacement tooth that is fitted on top. Because the implant stands on its own, the healthy teeth on either side do not need to be reshaped to support it, which is one of the main differences from a bridge.

People choose implants because:

  • They feel secure. An implant is fixed in place, so there is nothing to take out at night.
  • They look natural. The visible tooth is designed to blend in with your smile.
  • They help you chew comfortably, so you can enjoy your favourite foods again.
  • They protect neighbouring teeth, which are left untouched.
  • They help preserve the jawbone in the area of the missing tooth, which can otherwise shrink over time.

Is an implant right for you?

Implants are not the right choice for everyone, and we will always tell you honestly. Things we consider include:

  • The health of your gums. Gum disease needs to be treated first.
  • The amount of bone in the area where the tooth is missing.
  • Your general health and habits. Smoking and some health conditions can affect healing, so tell us about your medical history and any medicines you take.
  • Your preferences and budget.

If an implant is not suitable, or simply not what you want, we can talk through alternatives such as crowns and bridges or dentures. Our guide on options to replace a missing tooth compares them.

What to expect

  1. Consultation. We listen to your goals, examine your mouth and assess whether an implant is suitable for you.
  2. Treatment plan. You receive a clear plan covering each stage, a realistic timeline and the full cost before anything starts. Implant treatment is completed over a period of time, because the bone needs to heal around the implant before the final tooth is fitted.
  3. Treatment. The implant is placed under local anaesthetic, which numbs the area so most patients feel pressure rather than pain. Once healing is complete, the replacement tooth is fitted.
  4. Aftercare. We explain how to look after the area as it heals and how to clean around your implant long term. Good brushing, flossing and regular check-ups are what keep an implant healthy.

Premium care, affordable pricing

The cost of a dental implant depends on:

  • How many teeth you need to replace.
  • The condition of your gums and bone, and whether any preparatory treatment is needed first.
  • The replacement tooth that is fitted on the implant.
  • The number of visits your treatment plan involves.

An implant is an investment, so quality matters. Cutting corners on planning or materials can lead to problems that are costly to put right. Our approach is premium care with uncompromising attention to detail, priced to be good value for a tooth replacement designed to serve you for many years. Frequently asked questions

Does getting an implant hurt?

The area is numbed with local anaesthetic, so most patients feel pressure rather than pain during placement. Some soreness and swelling for a few days afterwards is common, and we will advise you on managing it.

How long does implant treatment take?

It is completed over several stages, because the bone needs time to heal around the implant. We give you a realistic timeline in your treatment plan.

Is an implant better than a bridge or denture?

Each option has advantages. Implants do not rely on neighbouring teeth and are fixed in place, while bridges and dentures may suit other situations or budgets. We will help you compare them honestly.

How do I look after an implant?

Much like a natural tooth: brush twice a day, clean between your teeth and keep up regular check-ups.
