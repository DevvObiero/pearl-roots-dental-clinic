Why do teeth
lose their shine?

Over time, dark beverages like coffee, tea, and red wine, along with lifestyle factors and natural aging, cause enamel to absorb deep pigments. Drugstore strips and abrasive powders often lead to uneven blotches, enamel erosion, and sharp nerve twinges.

At Pearl Roots Dental Clinic, our clinical treatments use professional-grade peroxide formulas balanced with desensitizing agents (potassium nitrate and fluoride). We break down deep-set stain compounds without damaging your enamel structure.

Extrinsic vs. Intrinsic Stains

Discoloration behaves differently depending on where it sits in the tooth structure:

  • Extrinsic stains: Rest on the enamel surface from coffee, spiced teas, tobacco, and dark sauces. These lift readily with peroxide bleaching agents.
  • Intrinsic stains: Reside in the inner dentin layer. These arise from aging, dental trauma, or childhood medication. They respond best to gradual tray courses or cosmetic bonding.

Debunking Common Myths

Myth

Whitening permanently erodes or thins your enamel.

Fact

Clinical whitening breaks pigment bonds inside microscopic enamel pores without altering tooth density or mineral volume.

Myth

Activated charcoal powder is a safe natural alternative.

Fact

Charcoal is abrasive and scratches the enamel surface, making it look yellower over time as inner dentin shows through.
