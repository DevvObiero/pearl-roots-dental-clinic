Preventive dental care at Pearl Roots Dental Clinic in Ngong, Nairobi is for every age: adults who want to keep their teeth for life, parents protecting their children's smiles, and anyone who has not seen a dentist in a while. We provide routine cleanings, screenings, splinting, mouth guards, fluoride therapy and pits and fissure sealants. Prevention is premium care that is also the most affordable dentistry there is, because problems caught early are simpler to treat.

## Treatments we offer

### Routine cleanings

Even with good brushing, plaque and hardened tartar build up in places a toothbrush cannot reach. A professional cleaning removes them, helps keep gums healthy and leaves your mouth feeling fresh. Cleanings can also help with some causes of bad breath; see our guide to [bad breath causes and treatment](/blog/bad-breath-causes-treatment/).

### Screenings

During a screening we check your teeth, gums and the soft tissues of your mouth for early signs of decay, gum disease and other changes. Spotting a small problem early usually means smaller, quicker and more cost-effective treatment.

### Splinting

If a tooth has become loose, for example after a knock or because of gum problems, splinting joins it to the neighbouring teeth for support while the area settles. We will explain whether splinting is right for you and what else may be needed.

### Mouth guards

A mouth guard cushions your teeth against impact. A custom-made guard is shaped to your own teeth, so it fits more comfortably than a generic one, which matters for anyone playing rugby, football, basketball or martial arts. Read more about [custom sports mouth guards](/blog/custom-sports-mouth-guard/).

### Fluoride therapy

Fluoride helps strengthen tooth enamel and makes it more resistant to decay. A professional fluoride application is a quick, simple step that can benefit children and adults who are prone to cavities.

### Pits and fissure sealants

The chewing surfaces of back teeth have deep grooves where food and bacteria collect. A sealant is a thin protective coating painted into those grooves to help keep decay out. Sealants are especially popular for children's newly erupted back teeth. Our guide on [fissure sealants and fluoride for children](/blog/fissure-sealants-fluoride-children/) explains how they work.

## What to expect

1. **Consultation.** We ask about your dental history, any concerns and your daily habits, then examine your teeth and gums.
2. **Treatment plan.** We explain what we found and which preventive treatments would help, along with how often to come back and what it will cost.
3. **Treatment.** Most preventive care is quick and comfortable. A cleaning, fluoride application or sealant is usually done in a single visit.
4. **Aftercare.** You leave with practical tips on brushing, flossing and diet, from cutting back on sugary chai between meals to choosing the right toothbrush, plus a sensible time for your next check-up.

## Premium care, affordable pricing

The cost of preventive care depends on:

- **Which services you need**, such as a cleaning alone or a cleaning with fluoride or sealants.
- **The current state of your teeth and gums**. Heavier tartar build-up can take more time.
- **How many teeth** are being sealed or treated.
- **Whether a custom item**, such as a mouth guard, is being made for you.

Skipping check-ups can feel like saving money, but a small cavity left alone can grow into a problem that needs a crown or even an extraction. Regular prevention is one of the best-value decisions you can make for your health. You will always know your costs upfront at your free consultation.

## Frequently asked questions

### How often should I have a check-up?

Most people benefit from regular check-ups, but the right interval depends on your own risk of decay and gum disease. We will recommend a schedule for you. Our article on [how often to get a dental check-up](/blog/how-often-dental-checkup/) covers this in more detail.

### Does a cleaning hurt?

Most people find a cleaning comfortable. If your gums are sensitive or inflamed you may feel some tenderness, and we will work gently and check in with you throughout.

### Are sealants and fluoride only for children?

They are very common for children, but adults who are prone to decay can benefit too. We will advise you at your consultation.
