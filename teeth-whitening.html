Why do teeth
lose their shine?

Over time, factors like coffee, tea, wine, smoking, and natural aging cause the enamel to dull and absorb stains. While over-the-counter strips offer temporary fixes, they often cause severe sensitivity and uneven results.

Our professional whitening protocols are formulated with desensitizing agents to safely break down deep molecular stains without compromising your enamel. We offer two distinct pathways to your brightest smile.
