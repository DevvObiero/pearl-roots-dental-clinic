Starting early gives every step time to settle and keeps the process calm rather than rushed. A smile makeover in Nairobi does not have to mean overspending, either. A clear plan lets you choose the treatments that matter most to you.

## What is a smile makeover?

A smile makeover is not one treatment. It is a personal plan that combines the options you need to reach the smile you want. Depending on your teeth, it might include:

- **Orthodontics**: braces or removable appliances to straighten teeth and close gaps
- **Composite bonding and masking**: tooth-coloured material to reshape chipped, uneven or discoloured teeth
- **Veneers**: thin covers on the front of teeth to change their shape, size or colour
- **Crowns, bridges or implants**: to restore damaged teeth or replace missing ones
- **Tooth-coloured fillings**: to replace dark old fillings that show when you smile
- **Teeth whitening**: in-clinic laser whitening or custom take-home trays

Some people need just one of these. Others benefit from a combination. Our [cosmetic dentistry](/services/cosmetic-dentistry/) page explains each option in more detail.

## Your wedding smile timeline

| Time before the wedding | What to focus on |
|---|---|
| 12 months or more | Consultation, check-up, any orthodontic treatment |
| 6 to 12 months | Treat any decay or gum problems, continue orthodontics |
| 3 to 6 months | Restorative and cosmetic work: bonding, veneers, crowns, bridges |
| 2 to 4 weeks | Teeth whitening |
| 1 to 2 weeks | Final check-up and cleaning |

### 12 months or more: start with the foundations

If you would like straighter teeth, orthodontic treatment is the step that takes longest, so it comes first. Treatment time varies with how much movement your teeth need, and some cases take longer than a year. Our guide to [fixed vs removable braces](/blog/fixed-vs-removable-braces/) explains the options.

This is also the time to deal with any decay, gum problems or teeth that need extracting, so the cosmetic work later is built on a healthy base.

### 3 to 6 months: restorative and cosmetic work

Once your teeth are in position and healthy, it is time for the work that shapes your smile:

- **Bonding** can often be completed in a single visit and is a gentle, conservative option for small chips and gaps.
- **Veneers** and **crowns** usually take more than one appointment, and it is wise to leave time for any adjustments.
- **Bridges or implants** for missing teeth need the most time, so raise them at your first consultation. Implants in particular involve a healing period of several months.

Not sure whether bonding or veneers suits you? Our comparison of [veneers vs composite bonding](/blog/veneers-vs-composite-bonding/) walks through the differences.

Whitening close to the wedding gives you the freshest result for your photos, while leaving time for any temporary sensitivity to settle. Our options are:

### 1 to 2 weeks before: a final check-up

A last visit lets us check that everything feels comfortable, give your teeth a polish and handle any small touch-ups. Avoid starting new treatments in the final week. Stick with what you know works.

## A premium look without overspending

A beautiful wedding smile does not have to be the most expensive line in your wedding budget. A few ways to get premium results at an affordable cost:

- **Prioritise what shows**: focus first on the teeth visible when you smile widely.
- **Choose conservative options where they work**: bonding or whitening can sometimes achieve the look you want without more involved treatment.
- **Start early**: rushing can limit your choices. Time gives you room to spread treatment out and choose carefully.
- **Avoid the cheapest shortcuts**: poorly made work can chip, stain or need replacing, which costs more in the long run. Uncompromising quality is the better investment for a smile you will keep long after the wedding.

At your free consultation, we give you a clear plan with options and costs before anything begins, so there are no surprises and no pressure.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it too late if my wedding is only a few months away?

Not at all. Orthodontics may not fit, but bonding, whitening, tooth-coloured fillings and some crowns or veneers often can. Book a consultation and we will tell you honestly what is realistic in the time you have.

### Can I wear braces at my wedding?

Yes, many people do. If you would rather not, ask us early about your options and timing.

### Should I whiten before or after veneers or bonding?

Before. Whitening only affects natural teeth, so we whiten first and then match any new restorations to your brighter shade.

### How much does a smile makeover cost?

It depends entirely on which treatments you need. Whitening starts from KSh 15,000 for a take-home kit. For everything else, you will receive a clear, personal quote at your free consultation.
