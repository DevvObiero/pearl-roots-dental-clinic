Restorative dentistry at Pearl Roots Dental Clinic in Ngong, Nairobi is for anyone with a decayed, broken or missing tooth who wants to eat, speak and smile comfortably again. We repair and replace teeth with tooth-coloured fillings, crowns, crowns and bridges, and partial or complete dentures. You get premium, carefully planned care at an affordable price, starting with a free consultation.

Treatments we offer

Tooth-coloured fillings

When decay or a small fracture damages a tooth, a filling seals and rebuilds it. We use tooth-coloured filling material that is matched to your natural shade, so the repair blends in rather than standing out when you laugh. Treating a cavity early with a filling is usually simpler and more affordable than waiting until the damage spreads. Learn more in our guide to tooth-coloured fillings in Nairobi.

Crowns

A crown covers and protects a tooth that is too weak or damaged for a filling alone, such as a tooth with a large old filling, a deep crack or heavy wear. It restores the tooth's shape and helps you chew with confidence again.

Crowns and bridges

If you have lost a tooth, a bridge can fill the gap. Crowns are placed on the teeth on either side of the space and support a replacement tooth between them. A bridge is fixed in place, so you do not take it out, and it helps stop neighbouring teeth drifting into the gap.

Partial dentures

A partial denture replaces one or several missing teeth while keeping your remaining natural teeth. It is removable, which makes cleaning straightforward, and it can make a real difference to chewing and to the look of your smile.

Complete dentures

When all the teeth in the upper or lower jaw are missing, a complete denture replaces the full arch. We take care over fit and appearance so your dentures feel as comfortable and natural as possible. Our guide on dentures cost in Kenya explains what to consider.

What to expect

  1. Consultation. We listen to your concerns, whether that is a sensitive tooth, a broken filling or a gap you are tired of hiding, and examine your teeth and gums.
  2. Treatment plan. We explain your options side by side. For a missing tooth, for example, that may mean comparing a bridge, a partial denture or a dental implant. You get a timeline and the cost before anything starts.
  3. Treatment. Local anaesthetic numbs the area where needed, so most patients feel pressure rather than pain. Crowns, bridges and dentures may take more than one visit so they fit properly.
  4. Aftercare. We show you how to care for your restoration, check your bite, and see you for follow-up visits so small issues are caught early.

Premium care, affordable pricing

What you pay for restorative care depends on:

  • The type of restoration. A filling, a crown, a bridge and a denture involve very different amounts of work and materials.
  • How many teeth need treatment.
  • The health of the surrounding teeth and gums, which may need attention first.
  • The number of visits needed to get a comfortable, accurate fit.

A cheap restoration that leaks, cracks or fits poorly can lead to new decay or the need to redo the work, which costs more in the end. We put uncompromising quality into every filling, crown and denture so it serves you well for years, while keeping our fees good value. At your free consultation, you hear exactly what your treatment will cost before it begins.

Frequently asked questions

Can you replace my old silver fillings with tooth-coloured ones?

Often, yes. If an old filling is worn, cracked or leaking, replacing it with a tooth-coloured filling is a common option. We will check whether it is advisable for your tooth.

Should I choose a bridge or a denture?

It depends on how many teeth are missing, the health of the neighbouring teeth and your preferences. We compare the options honestly at your consultation. Our article on options to replace a missing tooth is a helpful starting point.

How long does it take to get used to dentures?

Most people need a little time to adjust to speaking and eating with new dentures. We are here to check the fit and make adjustments if anything feels uncomfortable.
