What Happens at a Free Dental Consultation in Ngong?

Thinking of seeing a dentist in Ngong, Nairobi? Here's exactly what happens at a free consultation at Pearl Roots, step by step, with no pressure.

21 October 2026 2 min read Ngong, Nairobi

If you have been putting off a dental visit, you are not alone. Many people wait because they are unsure what will happen, what it will cost, or whether they will be pushed into treatment. That is exactly why we offer a free consultation at Pearl Roots Dental Clinic in Ngong, Nairobi: a calm, no-obligation first visit where you get answers before you decide anything.

Step 1: We listen first

Every visit starts with a conversation. Tell us what is bothering you, whether that's a sensitive tooth, a smile you'd like to brighten, crooked teeth or simply a check-up you have been postponing. We will ask about your dental history and any concerns you have about treatment.

Step 2: A gentle examination

Next, we take a careful look at your teeth and gums. The goal is simply to understand where your oral health is today and what, if anything, needs attention.

Step 3: A clear plan and your options

You leave with a clear explanation of what we found, the treatment options that fit your goals, a realistic timeline and next steps. If you need nothing more than routine care, we will tell you that too.

There is no obligation to start treatment on the day. Take the plan home, think it over, and reach out on WhatsApp whenever you are ready.

What can you ask about?

A consultation is a good time to ask about any of the treatments we offer, including:

  • Teeth whitening: in-clinic treatment or take-home kits (read our full whitening guide)
  • Braces and orthodontics: fixed and removable appliances, and retainers
  • Cosmetic dentistry: bonding, veneers and crowns
  • Restorative care: fillings, crowns and bridges, partial or complete dentures
  • Dental implants for long-term tooth replacement
  • Children's dentistry: gentle care to build healthy habits early

How to book

Booking takes less than a minute. Message us on WhatsApp or call +254 748 704 333. We are open Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm, in Ngong, Nairobi.
