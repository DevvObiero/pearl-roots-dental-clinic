Children's dentistry at Pearl Roots Dental Clinic in Ngong, Nairobi is for babies, young children and teenagers, and for parents who want their child's visits to be calm and positive. We provide gentle check-ups, preventive treatments and, when needed, treatment for problem teeth, all explained in a way children understand. It is premium paediatric care at a price that is affordable for families, starting with a free consultation.

Treatments we offer

Check-ups and cleanings

Regular visits let us follow how your child's teeth and jaws are developing and spot small problems early, before they cause pain. A gentle cleaning removes build-up and helps keep gums healthy. Early, friendly visits also help children see the dentist as a normal part of life. Not sure what happens the first time? Read our guide to your child's first dental visit.

Fluoride therapy

A professional fluoride application helps strengthen young enamel and makes teeth more resistant to decay. It is quick and simple, and most children cope with it easily.

Pits and fissure sealants

Sealants are thin protective coatings painted into the deep grooves of the back teeth, where food and bacteria tend to collect. They are a popular way to help protect newly erupted adult molars. Our article on fissure sealants and fluoride for children explains more.

Mouth guards

If your child plays contact sports at school or in a club, a custom mouth guard helps protect their teeth from knocks and falls during games and training.

Extractions for children

Sometimes a baby tooth needs to be removed, for example when it is badly decayed or not making way for the adult tooth. We do this gently and explain each step to both you and your child. See our tooth extraction page for more.

Braces and orthodontic appliances

As your child grows, we keep an eye on how their teeth and bite are developing. If we think they may benefit from braces or an orthodontic appliance, we will explain the options on our orthodontics page and discuss the right timing with you.

What to expect

  1. Consultation. We meet your child, let them get comfortable, and take time to listen to your questions. Then we gently examine their teeth and gums.
  2. Treatment plan. We explain what we found in simple terms, recommend any preventive or other care, and tell you the cost before anything starts.
  3. Treatment. We work at your child's pace, telling them what we are doing before we do it. Where numbing is needed, local anaesthetic numbs the area so most children feel pressure rather than pain.
  4. Aftercare. We share practical tips for home: brushing twice a day, helping younger children brush until they can do it well, and cutting down on sugary snacks and drinks between meals.

Tips to help your child feel relaxed

  • Talk about the visit in a positive, matter-of-fact way.
  • Avoid words like "hurt" or "injection" that can create worry.
  • Book a time when your child is usually rested and settled.
  • Bring a favourite toy if it helps them feel secure.

Premium care, affordable pricing

The cost of your child's dental care depends on:

  • The type of visit, such as a routine check-up or treatment for a specific problem.
  • Which preventive treatments are recommended, such as fluoride or sealants.
  • How many teeth need attention.

Prevention in childhood is one of the most cost-effective choices a family can make. Treating a small cavity early, or preventing it altogether, is far simpler and better value than dealing with pain and bigger treatment later. We offer premium care with a gentle touch, and you will always know the costs upfront at your free consultation.

Frequently asked questions

When should my child first see a dentist?

A good guide is around the time the first tooth appears, or by their first birthday. Early visits are mostly about getting comfortable and giving parents advice.

Do you treat nervous children?

Yes. We take things slowly, explain each step and let children ask questions. Tips for anxious patients of any age are in our guide to dental anxiety.

Can I book around school?

We are open Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm, so many parents book during school holidays or later in the afternoon. Message us on WhatsApp or call +254 748 704 333.
