Orthodontic treatment at Pearl Roots Dental Clinic in Ngong, Nairobi is for children, teenagers and adults with crooked, crowded or gapped teeth, or a bite that does not meet comfortably. We offer braces, fixed and removable orthodontic appliances, and fixed and removable retainers, combining premium care with pricing that is affordable for families. It all begins with a free consultation where we look at your bite and explain your options.

## Treatments we offer

### Braces

Braces apply gentle, steady pressure that gradually moves teeth into better positions. They can help straighten crooked teeth, close gaps, relieve crowding and improve how your upper and lower teeth fit together. Straighter teeth are not only about looks: they are often easier to brush and floss properly, which supports long-term oral health.

### Fixed orthodontic appliances

Fixed appliances stay in the mouth throughout treatment and are adjusted by us at your review visits. Because they cannot be taken out, they work continuously and do not rely on you remembering to wear them, which makes them a dependable choice for many patients, including busy students and working adults.

### Removable orthodontic appliances

Removable appliances can be taken out for eating and cleaning. They can suit certain simpler tooth movements, and they are often used for younger patients whose jaws are still growing. They only work when worn as instructed, so we will be honest about whether a removable appliance is realistic for you or your child. Our guide on [fixed vs removable braces](/blog/fixed-vs-removable-braces/) compares the two in more detail.

### Fixed and removable retainers

Once teeth have moved, they naturally try to drift back. A retainer holds them in their new position. A fixed retainer is a discreet wire bonded behind the teeth, while a removable retainer is worn as directed and taken out for cleaning. We will recommend the type that best protects your result and explain how long to wear it.

## What to expect

Orthodontic treatment is a journey of months rather than days, so a clear plan matters:

1. **Consultation.** We listen to what bothers you about your teeth or bite and examine your teeth, gums and jaw. We check that teeth and gums are healthy enough to begin.
2. **Treatment plan.** You receive a clear plan: which appliance we recommend and why, a realistic timeline, how often you will need review visits and the full cost. For school-going children we can plan around school terms where possible.
3. **Treatment.** We fit your braces or appliance and see you regularly to adjust it and track progress. Some tightness or soreness for a few days after fitting and adjustments is normal and usually settles.
4. **Aftercare.** When active treatment ends, we fit your retainer and explain how to wear and clean it, because retention is what keeps your new smile in place.

## Premium care, affordable pricing

## Frequently asked questions

### Am I too old for braces?

Many adults straighten their teeth successfully. What matters most is the health of your teeth and gums, which we check at your consultation.

### Do braces hurt?

Braces should not be painful, but most people feel some pressure or soreness for a few days after fitting and after adjustments. Soft foods help during that time, and it usually eases quickly.

### How long will I need braces?

It varies with how much your teeth need to move. We give you a realistic timeline in your treatment plan rather than a one-size-fits-all answer.

### Why do I need a retainer after braces?

Teeth tend to drift back towards their old positions. Wearing your retainer as advised is the best way to protect your result.
