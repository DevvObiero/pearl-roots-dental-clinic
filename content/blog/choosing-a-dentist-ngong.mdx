Choosing a dentist in Ngong comes down to a few things you can check for yourself: whether they explain things clearly, tell you costs upfront, keep a visibly clean clinic, offer the treatments you are likely to need, help you feel comfortable, fit your location and schedule, and let you meet them before you commit. A good dentist is someone you are happy to return to for years, so it is worth taking a little time over the decision.

Here are the seven things we believe matter most, and what to look for in each.

## 1. Clear explanations in plain language

You should leave every appointment understanding what is happening in your mouth and why. A good dentist:

- Explains what they found without jargon
- Shows you the problem where possible
- Gives you more than one option when more than one exists, including the option to wait and monitor
- Is happy to answer questions, even ones you think are silly

If you come away confused or feeling rushed into a decision, that is a sign to ask more questions before agreeing to treatment.

## 2. Upfront, honest costs

Nobody likes a surprise at the end of a visit. Before any treatment starts, you should know what it will cost and what is included. Look for a dentist who:

- Gives you a clear quote after examining you
- Explains what drives the cost, such as materials, the number of visits and complexity
- Tells you when a cheaper option may cost more in the long run
- Does not pressure you to decide on the spot

At Pearl Roots, our approach is premium dental care at an affordable price. You learn your options and their costs before treatment begins, so you can plan with confidence.

## 3. Visible hygiene and infection control

You cannot see everything that happens behind the scenes, but you can notice a lot:

- Clean, tidy treatment rooms and waiting areas
- Fresh gloves and a mask for each patient
- Instruments opened in front of you from sealed packaging, or clearly prepared for you
- Surfaces wiped down between patients

It is perfectly reasonable to ask how instruments are cleaned and sterilised. A confident clinic will be glad to explain.

## 5. Comfort and a gentle approach

Many adults put off the dentist because of a bad experience years ago. A caring dental team takes this seriously. Things to look for:

- They ask how you feel about treatment before they start
- They explain each step as they go
- They agree a signal you can use to pause
- Local anaesthetic numbs the area for procedures, so most patients feel pressure rather than pain
- They are patient and kind with children

If nerves are holding you back, read our guide to [managing dental anxiety](/blog/dental-anxiety-ngong/).

No. Regular check-ups catch problems early, when treatment is usually simpler and more affordable.
